Primary surface
Target language fills the screen
The translation is what you read. Source is always one tap away — never in the way.
Paragraph peek
Inline source reveal per paragraph
Tap the peek button beneath any paragraph to pull up the original inline — without losing your place.
Word tap
Definition sheet from the bottom
Tap any word to get IPA, part of speech, contextual definition, and audio — in a thumb-friendly bottom sheet.
Tab switcher
Full-column language flip
Switch between French-first and English-first reading in one tap. Useful for different study modes.
Navigation
Swipe or tap to turn pages
Left/right swipe for pages. Blur mode toggle and page counter live in the persistent bottom bar.
Why not vertical stack?
Stacking source and target vertically means constant scrolling up and down to compare. Peek-per-paragraph keeps comparison local and instant.
Blur still works
The blur mode applies to the peeked source text — so word/sentence/paragraph blur carries over naturally to mobile without any redesign.
One shared codebase
The reader component detects viewport width. Below 640px it switches to the mobile layout. No separate app needed — responsive CSS handles it.
